V1.9 AI component

Primary copy-detection descriptor: Meta Research SSCD
Project: A Self-Supervised Descriptor for Image Copy Detection (CVPR 2022)
Model: sscd_disc_mixup TorchScript, 512-dimensional L2-normalized descriptor
License: MIT (upstream codebase)

The model binary is NOT bundled in this ZIP. Railway downloads the official pretrained TorchScript model on first startup and stores it under /data/models when the persistent Volume is mounted.

This bot uses SSCD for same-photo / edited-copy detection. It does not perform face identity recognition.
